FOR EVERYDAY ACCESS

Hot storage

For active projects, frequently accessed files, and data you work with every day.

3replicas
  • Three copies across logical placement zones
  • SHA-256 verification when retrieving data
  • Configurable replication and quorum
  • Replica repair through integrity scans
FOR THE LONGER TERM

Cold storage

For archives, snapshots, and data where reduced storage overhead matters.

10 + 4data + parity fragments
  • About 1.4× storage before fragment padding
  • Reconstruct from ten valid fragments
  • Individual fragment checksum verification
  • Automatic repair of missing fragments
